Artificial intelligence is transforming education in several ways. Intelligent tutoring systems can personalize learning by adapting explanations, examples, and difficulty levels to each student. Teachers can use AI-powered analytics to identify learners who need support, monitor progress over time, and design targeted interventions. Administrative tasks such as grading objective assessments, organizing schedules, and answering common student questions can also be automated, allowing teachers to spend more time on mentorship and instruction.

At the same time, the use of AI in education introduces challenges. Schools must think carefully about data privacy, bias in automated decisions, and the risk of students over-relying on generated answers instead of developing their own understanding. Educators also need training so they can use these tools responsibly and explain their limitations to students. When implemented thoughtfully, AI can expand access to learning resources, improve efficiency, and support more inclusive educational experiences.
